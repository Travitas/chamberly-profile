About Adv. Varun Jain

Adv. Varun Jain is the Founder of Jain Law & Company, delivering strategic legal solutions across Real Estate & RERA, Corporate & Commercial Disputes, NCLT / IBC insolvency, and High Court Litigation.

With over 14+ years of courtroom experience in Delhi courts and regulatory tribunals, he approaches legal matters by understanding not only the legal issues but also the commercial and procedural considerations surrounding them.

His practice specializes in protecting client interests through proactive pre-litigation advisory, effective dispute mitigation, contractual safeguarding, and strategic representation before various appellate forums and High Courts.
